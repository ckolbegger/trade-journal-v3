## Repair to verify (first critic pass)
The first pass found that available replay sessions showed underlying prices (`100.00`, `102.00`) instead of the exact option Marks (`3.00`, `3.80`), and advisory IV fell into the expiration-day `Unavailable` branch with share equivalent `200` and a zero-move decomposition. Both shared one root cause: `contractOf()` in `src/ui/detail-controller.ts` read a top-level `strategyId` that `confirmOptionPlan` freezes only inside the Plan. It now resolves the Strategy id from `plan.strategyId` as well. Re-check flows 2 and 3 explicitly: session points must be `2026-11-20 · 3.00` and `2026-11-23 · 3.80`, and advisory IV must be a deterministic numeric value (not an `Unavailable` reason) with a non-zero decomposition.
