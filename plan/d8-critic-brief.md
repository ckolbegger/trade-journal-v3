A single option leg opens through one Position Change with the frozen exact contract and one reflection outcome, and closes through one terminal Position Change with one Close Reason, FIFO Lot Matches and realized P&L. Option exposure is not shown on the stock Marks/Risk card.

## Flows
1. **Strategy selection and live Baseline.** Confirm the four selectors are exactly Long Stock, Long Call, Long Put, Cash-Secured Put; Long Stock is the default and still previews 1R `50.00` / R:R `2.00` for 10 @ 100 / 95 / 110. No spread/settlement/replay/provider/report control is visible anywhere.
2. **Incomplete contract writes nothing.** For an option shape leave Strike empty, fill everything else, click Confirm plan: visible prose names `Strike`, the app stays on the form, and `indexedDB` `trade` store contains no row starting `trade-aapl`.
3. **Exact frozen contract and distinct Trade identity.** Confirm one Long Call. Inspect the stored `trade` row: `lifecycle: Planned`, `strategyId: seed-long-call`, `contract` with underlying AAPL, kind Call, strike 190, expiration 2026-12-18, multiplier 100; `plannedLeg.openingAction: Buy`; `oneR: 400.00`; journal `entry-<id>` exists. Confirm a second option Plan on the same underlying the same day and verify the two Trade ids differ (no same-day same-underlying collision).
4. **Option open/close end to end and durability.** On the Long Call, Add execution with the exact contract, defer, Record position change: Open detail shows contract `AAPL 190Call 2026-12-18 x100`, one Lot, allocated opening fee `1.00`, net position `2`, realized `0.00`; exactly one `PositionChange:` reflection outcome row. Close the position with the exact contract and Target Reached: Closed detail shows realized P&L `398.00`, one FIFO Lot Match, Close Reason `Target Reached`, lifecycle Closed, and the Closed index contains the id while the Open index no longer does. Reload, reopen the Closed detail: the same figures persist.
5. **Independent intrinsic payoff.** On a confirmed Plan's detail the payoff panel shows break-even `193`, the minimum `-600.00` on `0..190`, an unbounded maximum from `193`, and explicitly no model-derived price. On the Open detail after the opening fill, the independent Current curve has the same break-even `193` and intercept `-600.00` because realized-to-date is `0.00` in this fixture. Confirm the panel changes with contract terms and is unaffected by any Marks history; no IV/theoretical input or control exists. (A synthetic `100.00` realized offset shifting the Current break-even to `192.5` is covered by the domain unit test, not by this UI fixture.)
6. **Long Put and short Cash-Secured Put direction.** Open one Long Put (opening action Buy) and one Cash-Secured Put (opening action Sell): the Open detail net position is `+2` and `-2` respectively and the stored opening Executions carry the correct action. Close the short put by buying to close; realized P&L is computed with the short-direction sign and the Trade closes flat with one Close Reason. No assignment/exercise/settlement fact is inferred.
