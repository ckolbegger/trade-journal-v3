# D9.5: proposed spec text changes

Agreed by the user on 2026-11-22 and made in `specs/` by plan decision 125 (revision 114). Each item gives the text as it stood ("Now") and the text as made ("Proposed").

Four items held a choice that the earlier answers did not settle, marked **Choice 1**, **Choice 2**, **Choice 3** and **Choice 4**. The user settled all four on 2026-11-22, each as recommended, so the text shown is the chosen text.

There are 21 items: Daily Review (items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8), the UI contract (item 9), the Journal (items 10, 11 and 12), Trade Workflows (item 13), the glossary (item 14) and the acceptance contract (items 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 and 21).

## Daily Review (`specs/design/daily-review.md`)

### 1. "Date, eligibility, and ordering": where Debt and settlement appear (Choice 4)

Now, after the four-item task-family list:

> Acknowledged Unavailable evidence is resolved but disclosed. Historical gaps are separately visible and do not block the current Review.

Proposed, one sentence added before it (the list itself is unchanged):

> A Trade's due Journal Debt and its settlement still due may be presented with that Trade inside the walk, ahead of its Action; the walk then ranks a Trade with settlement due by the bands below (plan decisions 29, 61 and 125).

Why: D5R already shows owed reflections inside the Trade's card (decisions 29 and 61, never written into the spec). D9.5 moves settlement into the card too, and ranks a Trade whose Exit was not carried out ahead of an expired contract.

### 2. "Date, eligibility, and ordering": the attention bands

Now:

> Attention uses transparent bands: factual/management blocker, boundary reached, evidence limited, quantified pressure, then routine. It returns reasons and inputs, not a stored severity or discipline score. Within a Review view, order is stable. A fresh `open` may produce a new order.

Proposed:

> Attention uses transparent bands: factual/management blocker, an Exit not carried out, settlement due, Stop reached, Target reached, evidence limited, quantified pressure, then routine. An Exit is not carried out when the Trade's most recent Daily Trade Review Action before the Review Date is Exit. Within the Stop-reached band, Trades sort by Stop Overrun in R, deepest first, ahead of the within-band order below. It returns reasons and inputs, not a stored severity or discipline score. Within a Review view, order is stable: saving an Action or resolving Debt does not reorder it. A fresh `open`, or a Marks save that returns a refreshed view, may produce a new order (plan decision 125).

The pressure paragraph that follows is unchanged.

### 3. `getTrade`: the Action form

Now:

> For a new Action, Hold is preselected only in returned view state. The visible Save button creates the one-click unchanged-Hold path. Opening, selecting, or navigating away records nothing. Exit, Roll, and Adjust require nonblank Intent. Saving any Action records intent only and may offer navigation to a Trade Workflow; it never executes or infers a Position Change.

Proposed:

> For a new Action, the returned view state follows the Plan Call (see "Following the Plan"): Hold is preselected only when the call is `Hold`, and nothing is preselected otherwise. With Hold preselected, the visible Save button creates the one-click unchanged-Hold path. Opening, selecting, or navigating away records nothing. Roll and Adjust require nonblank Intent; Hold and Exit require it as "Following the Plan" states. Saving any Action records intent only. The Review may then present the Trade Workflow's own form in place, or offer navigation to it; the Action itself never executes or infers a Position Change, and a Position Change is recorded only by Trade Workflows (plan decision 125).

### 4. A new section, "Following the Plan", placed after `getTrade`

Proposed:

> ## Following the Plan
>
> The Plan Exit is the Plan's own exit: the whole open Position, when a headline Stop or Target is reached. The Plan Call for a Review Date is derived each time from `evaluate` at that date's Marks and is never stored: `Out` when a headline Stop is Breached or a headline Target is Reached; `Hold` when every stated headline condition is evaluable and none is reached; otherwise `Unknown`.
>
> Saving an Action is refused, with a typed no-write result, while a current Mark the Trade requires is Missing. An Unavailable acknowledgment resolves the Mark and leaves the call `Unknown`.
>
> Intent follows the call. Hold requires nonblank Intent when the call is `Out`. Exit requires it unless the call is `Out`. Roll and Adjust always require it. Daily Review enforces this at Save; the Entry Definition requires Intent only for Roll and Adjust. The rule records nothing but the Intent and creates no Deviation (plan decision 125).

Not in the agreed answers, assumed here: when the call is `Unknown` (a Mark acknowledged Unavailable), nothing is preselected, a Hold needs no Intent and an Exit needs one.

### 5. `save`: Settle Later

Now (unchanged, shown for place):

> The Action always has Source Daily Review, a Trade Anchor, and Daily Review origin `(Trade, Review Date)`. …

Proposed, one sentence added at the end of that paragraph:

### 6. "Settlement Due"

Now:

> When an option reaches a factual settlement point without an explicit outcome, Review reports Settlement Due and remains incomplete. Only Trade Workflows may record Expiration, Assignment, Exercise, or cash settlement. The calendar never manufactures Expiration.

Proposed:

> When an option reaches a factual settlement point without an explicit outcome, Review reports Settlement Due and remains incomplete until the outcome is recorded or the trader saves Settle Later for that Trade and Review Date. Only Trade Workflows may record Expiration, Assignment, Exercise, or cash settlement; the Review may present that workflow's form in place. The calendar never manufactures Expiration.
>
> A Trade with Settlement Due owes no Hold, Exit, Roll, or Adjust for that Review Date. Settle Later is the trader's explicit statement that an Assignment or Exercise will be recorded later. It is available only while the installed release cannot record that outcome. It is saved as that date's Daily Trade Review Entry, creates no settlement fact, and resolves the settlement obligation for that Review Date only: the next Review reports Settlement Due again (plan decision 125).

The second paragraph of the section and its diagram are unchanged.

### 7. "Completion and resumption"

Now:

> - `Incomplete` while an eligible Trade lacks a saved Action, a required current Mark is Missing, due Debt remains, settlement/management is due, a deterministic Deviation awaits reconciliation, or integrity prevents a coherent result;
> - `Complete` when every obligation is resolved with exact current Marks;
> - `CompleteWithUnavailableMarks` when all obligations are resolved but at least one required Instrument has only an explicit unavailable acknowledgment. The result lists Instruments, Trades, and affected calculation families.

Proposed:

> - `Incomplete` while an eligible Trade that owes an Action lacks a saved one, a required current Mark is Missing, due Debt remains, settlement is due with neither a recorded outcome nor a Settle Later for that date, management is due, a deterministic Deviation awaits reconciliation, or integrity prevents a coherent result;
> - `Complete` when every obligation is resolved with exact current Marks and no Settle Later;
> - `CompleteWithUnavailableMarks` when all obligations are resolved but at least one required Instrument has only an explicit unavailable acknowledgment. The result lists Instruments, Trades, and affected calculation families;
> - `CompleteWithSettlementToRecord` when all obligations are resolved but at least one Trade's settlement is resolved only by Settle Later. The result lists those Trades. When unavailable acknowledgments also apply, the result carries both disclosures (plan decision 125).

### 8. "Default Action definition"

Now:

> The initial Daily Trade Review Action options are Hold, Exit, Roll, and Adjust. Hold must remain available while the one-click capability is installed. Hold has optional Intent; the other three require Intent. “Watch Closely” is not an Action. Missing Action is never inferred as Hold.

Proposed:

> The initial Daily Trade Review Action options are Hold, Exit, Roll, and Adjust, plus the protected option Settle Later, which is saved only as "Settlement Due" states and is never offered among the four. Hold must remain available while the one-click capability is installed. Roll and Adjust require Intent; Hold and Exit require it as "Following the Plan" states. “Watch Closely” is not an Action. Missing Action is never inferred as Hold (plan decision 125).

## Trade Workflows (`specs/design/trade-workflows.md`)

### 13. `recordPositionChange`: the Plan check

Now (unchanged, shown for place):

> The presentation may preselect Defer for Position Change Reflection and terminal Close Review so factual capture remains low-friction, but the submitted command always carries the disposition explicitly. No abandoned form creates Debt.

Proposed, a new paragraph after it:

> A change that reduces or closes a Position by Execution is measured against the Plan Exit. It follows the Plan Exit when it closes the whole Position, a headline Stop or Target is reached at the Trade's latest Marks (or, for a condition priced in the traded Instrument, at the change's own price), and no Hold Action was saved on an earlier Review Date while that condition was reached. Otherwise it leaves the Plan Exit: before a condition is reached, after holding past one, or by closing only part. Such a change, and an Expiration, submit Complete for the Position Change Reflection and Defer for a terminal Close Review; the presentation offers no other disposition for them. A change that leaves the Plan Exit requires the Plan Departure Reason, unless it closes the whole Position and the Trade's most recent Daily Trade Review Action is an Exit with nonblank Intent. An Expiration recorded while the contract is in the money at the session's Underlying Mark requires the Expiration Reason. The measure is derived each time and stored nowhere; it is not a Deviation (plan decision 125).

A buy is not measured and keeps today's reflection choices.

## Glossary (`specs/glossary.md`)

### 14. Three entries added to "Review and reporting language" (Choice 3)

Decision 69 lets this branch add glossary entries and never change one. The proposal keeps that rule: three entries are added and no existing entry or invariant changes.

> | **Plan Exit** | The Plan's own exit: the whole open Position, when a headline Stop or Target is reached. An Action or a sale is measured against it; the result is derived and never stored. Added by plan decision 125. | Exit strategy, trailing plan |
> | **Plan Call** | What the Plan Exit calls for on one session's Marks: `Out` when a headline Stop is Breached or a headline Target is Reached, `Hold` when neither is, `Unknown` when they cannot be evaluated. Added by plan decision 125. | Advice, signal, recommendation |
> | **Settle Later** | The protected Daily Trade Review option by which the trader states that an Assignment or Exercise will be recorded later. It resolves Settlement Due for one Review Date only and is never a settlement fact. Added by plan decision 125. | Skip, snooze, inferred Expiration |

The invariant "A **Daily Review** is complete only when all eligible Trade Actions, current Mark resolutions, due Debt, settlement, management, and deterministic reconciliation obligations are resolved" stays as written. It is read with the new entries: an expired contract owes a settlement, not an Action, and Settle Later resolves that settlement for one Review Date.

### 19. New: AC-REV-007, after AC-REV-006

> ### AC-REV-007 — the Action follows the Plan
>
> - **Given:** three eligible Trades with current Marks, one past its Stop, one at its Target and one inside both, and a fourth with a Missing current Mark.
> - **When:** each Action form opens, and Hold and then Exit are saved with and without Intent.
> - **Then:** Hold is preselected only for the Trade inside both. For the two whose Plan Call is `Out`, nothing is preselected, Hold without Intent rejects and Exit without Intent saves. For the Trade inside both, Exit without Intent rejects. No Action saves for the fourth Trade until its Mark is entered or acknowledged Unavailable. Every saved Action records intent only; the rule creates no Deviation or other Trade fact.
> - **Evidence:** returned form state, validation results, Entry snapshots, and unchanged Trade facts.

### 20. New: AC-REV-008, after AC-REV-007

> ### AC-REV-008 — a sale is measured against the Plan Exit
>
> - **Given:** an Open Trade with a saved Exit Action, in four cases: the whole Position sold with its Stop reached and never held past; the whole Position sold before either condition is reached, with Intent on the Exit Action; the same with no Intent; and part of the Position sold.
> - **When:** the sale is recorded from Daily Review and from Trade Detail.
> - **Then:** each sale is one Position Change recorded by Trade Workflows with a completed Position Change Reflection and no reflection Debt. The first and second cases ask for no reason; the third and fourth reject without a Plan Departure Reason. A terminal sale requires its Close Reason and leaves one Outstanding Close Review Debt. The measure is stored nowhere and creates no Deviation.
> - **Evidence:** command validation, Trade receipts, Journal effects, and the same outcomes from both entry points.

### 21. New: AC-SET-005, after AC-SET-004

> ### AC-SET-005 — Settle Later finishes one Review only
>
> - **Given:** an in-the-money option past expiration with Settlement Due, on a release that cannot record its Assignment or Exercise, and every other obligation resolved.
> - **When:** the trader saves Settle Later, and the next Review Date is then opened.
> - **Then:** one Daily Trade Review Entry with Settle Later exists for that Trade and date and no Trade fact changes. That Review is Complete With Settlement To Record and names the Trade. The Trade owes no Hold, Exit, Roll, or Adjust on either date. The next Review reports Settlement Due again and is Incomplete.
> - **Evidence:** before/after facts, Entry snapshot, and both completion results.

## The four choices (settled by the user, 2026-11-22, each as recommended)

1. **Position Change Reflection's three existing prompts** (item 10). Recommended: they become optional and sit behind "Add more" on the sale and expiration forms; a buy still shows them, now skippable. Other ways: retire them, or keep them required and shown on a sale.
2. **Prompt labels** (items 10 and 12). The UI must show the form's own prompt label, and the mockup shows different labels by situation ("Why leave the Plan?", "Why did you leave the Plan?", "Why only part?", "Why? (optional)"). Recommended: the Action's prompt is renamed "Why?" and a line above it says how the Plan is being left; a part sale uses "Why did you leave the Plan?" under "Leaves the Plan: only part".
3. **The glossary** (item 14). Recommended: add three entries and change none, keeping decision 69's rule. Other way: lift the rule and rewrite the completion invariant.
4. **Where Debt and settlement appear** (items 1 and 15). Recommended: write into the spec that they appear with their Trade, which lets an Exit not carried out rank ahead of an expired contract. Other way: keep the spec's order and put expired contracts first.
