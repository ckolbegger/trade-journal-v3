## 1. Deviation D-001 — the Plan becomes previewable before it is frozen

**What the user asked for, and why it is a deviation.** D2 delivered the confirmation form without
showing the trader the planned reward-to-risk ratio or the maximum loss until *after* Save. The user's
judgement, in session:

> "I definitely want the trader to see R:R and maximum loss BEFORE saving the plan, because the plan
> should be immutable (or at least somewhat resistant to change). … I don't think we should wait for
> D4."

That reasoning is sound and I record it as the justification: a figure the trader can only see once it
is immutable cannot inform the decision to freeze it. D3 is *"Record my entry (long stock)"* — fills,
lots, FIFO, realized P&L, `Planned`→`Open` — so the change does not belong there either.

Disclosing it needs Trade Analysis's derivation to be reachable from the form, and
`scripts/layers.config.mjs:70` does not permit presentation to import analysis. That requires a
UI-callable coordinator operation, and `trade-workflows.md` fixes exactly six. Adding a seventh is a
public-behaviour and capability-scope change, which §8 classes as a **deviation** rather than an
errata or adjustment, with a new freeze revision — this record.
