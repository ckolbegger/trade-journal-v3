**In the trader's words.**
- **The Review becomes three steps on one page:** Marks, Trades, the day. The top card says what is left and one button leads to the next thing.
- **Marks are typed once, at the top,** for every Instrument. A price that looks like a slip is questioned before it is saved. One Save Marks, then the grid folds away.
- **Each Trade's card sets the Plan beside now,** with the last decisions next to the choice.
- **The Plan makes the call.** Inside the lines Hold is one press. Past the Stop or at the Target nothing is chosen for you, and a Hold needs a reason.
- **A sale is recorded in the Review** and measured against the Plan's exit. Only a sale that leaves the Plan asks why.
- **An expired contract is settled in the Review,** or put off with "record later" when it was exercised or assigned.
- **The Replay sits on every card** and shows what was decided each day. A closed Trade's Close Review is written after walking its key moments.
- **All of it works from the keyboard and on a phone.**

## 2. Boundaries

- **Trade Analysis** derives the Plan Call, an expired contract's standing and the key moments. It reads no Journal and stores nothing (P165, P172). The measure of a sale is a pure function in `src/domain/plan-exit/`, enforced by Trade Workflows (P165, question Q9).
- **Daily Review** owns the page's view, the day summary, the order, the save rules and Settle Later. It writes Journal entries and Marks only; it never writes a Trade fact.
- **Trade Workflows** alone records a sale or an expiration, and alone enforces the measure and the dispositions, from the Review and from Trade Detail alike (AC-REV-008).
- **The Journal** validates a form against its revision and seeds revisions. It knows nothing of the Plan Call.
- **Market Data** is unchanged. The slip check is not its concern (P167) and no correction rule of its own changes (answer 8).
- **The screens** show what the views return. They hold drafts, slip answers, "Still holding" and the seen key moments as screen state, never saved (AC-JOUR-001).
- **Not in D9.5:** spreads (D10), fetched Marks (D11), earlier Review dates (D15), recording an exercise or assignment (D20), forms for Roll and Adjust (D21, D17), a Settings page (D23), any change to Trade Detail beyond the shared words, order, exit form and Close Review walk.
