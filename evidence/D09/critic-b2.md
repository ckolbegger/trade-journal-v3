## 4. Minor observations

1. **The small chart has no scale, dates or values, and no "estimate" tag of its own.** It is a bare dashed line that stretches to fill its box. So BREN's drift from 32.0% to 29.5% looks as steep as CORV's rise from 39.9% to 53.0%. The values only reach a screen reader, through the hidden list ("Mon Nov 2: 32.0% …").
   - The prototype's pop-up (`prototypes/replay/round-2.html`, `ivChart(pw, 120, c, true)`) has a heading with the tag, percent labels on an axis, a cursor, and the caption "Read back from each session's Marks… A gap means none could be read."
   - No spec line or approved wording requires these, so I am not counting it as a defect. It is the stage B item most likely to mislead.
2. **The share equivalent never says why it is Unavailable.** On an Unavailable session its chip disappears, and the readout and session card say "Behaves like Unavailable". Only the Implied volatility chip carries the reason. AC-CALC-005 says "each is Unavailable with a stable reason". The reason it shares with Implied volatility is fair, but the share equivalent never shows it.
3. **The share equivalent chip opens no small chart.** `research/replay-requirements.md` and the prototype have one. Plan T9.24 asks only for the volatility chip's, so this is within the plan.
4. **The chips' small chart opens on focus, click and Enter, but not on hover.** T9.24 lists only those three. The prototype and the requirements note also mention hover.
5. **At 1280 px the open small chart covers the lower left of the Trade P&amp;L chart and its key.** It is a pop-up and closes with Escape, so this is cosmetic.
6. **Exact sentences on flat sessions.** BREN Wed Nov 4 reads "Trade P&amp;L $0.00. The call moved $0.00 a share: BREN's move +$0.11 · time −$0.08 · volatility −$0.03". The arithmetic is consistent. The readout's matching line, "up $0.00 since Tue Nov 3", comes from stage A, not stage B.
