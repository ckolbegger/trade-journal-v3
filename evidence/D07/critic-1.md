### 3. Overall: PASS

Every pass condition is met. Three defects in what is shown on screen:

1. **Two screens describe an option Trade's Terminal Disposition with the stock wording ("shares").** Severity: Moderate. The wording is wrong, and on the owed card the mechanism is wrong too (it misreports an expiration as a sale).
   - Steps: (1) On an Open Long Put with 2 contracts, record a Sell to close of 2 contracts. The exit receipt shows "Terminal Disposition: Closed via sale of 2 shares". (2) In the C7.6 fixture, record T-1's expiration with the Close Review deferred, then open the Review. The owed card in "Trades with owed reflections" shows "Terminal Disposition: Closed via sale of 2 shares".
   - Expected: approved words row 23.12 ("Detail, receipts, Owed card"): "Closed via sale of 2 contracts", and for T-1 "Closed via sale of 1 contract and expiration of 1 contract". Convention C1. A108.
   - Actual: Trade Detail and the expiration receipt are correct. The likely cause is that `src/ui/screens/ExitReceipt.tsx:146` and `src/ui/screens/ReviewOwedCard.tsx:38` still call the stock-only `closedViaSale` (`src/ui/figures.ts:11`).
2. **This is the owed-card half of Defect 1, as it shows in C7.6.** Severity: Moderate, already counted under Defect 1. The trader reviewing a Trade that partly expired is told it was closed by a sale of "2 shares". That contradicts A108 / A119 and Terminal Disposition "from actual mechanisms" (AC-POS-004).
3. **Payoff chart labels collide, and at narrow widths the chart is illegible.** Severity: Minor. The table is the full text equivalent and stays readable.
   - At 1280 px: C7.1 Planned: the tick labels "55.00" and "57.00" overprint as "55700". MNO Cash-Secured Put Planned: the tick labels "43.80" and "45.00" overprint, and "Breakeven 43.80" overprints "stays at +$240.00".
   - At 375 and 320 px the same collisions remain and the chart's text scales down to roughly 5px.
   - In the 320 px Current table the sign of "−$101.95" wraps onto its own line.
   - Expected: A114 says zero crossings and extremum sets are "marked and labelled". AC-UI-002 asks for readable charts.
