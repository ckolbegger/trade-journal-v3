- **Expected:** Plan P25 and A110: after the expiration date "no Mark is required for it or for its Underlying's conditions, and every current figure is `Unavailable(SettlementDue)`". A115: Market Data rejects an option Mark dated after expiration (`AfterExpiration`), so such a Mark can never be supplied. `specs/design/ui-contract.md` line 56: settlement due and Missing Marks "appear under their own semantics". The Review card for the same Trades correctly says "No Marks for Mon Nov 23".
- **Actual:** the Valuation section opens with "Mark missing for XYZ 20 Nov 2026 55 Call on Mon Nov 23" (T-3: "Mark missing for MNO 20 Nov 2026 45 Put on Mon Nov 23"), while every figure under it reads "Settlement Due: …". Seen on the production and faults builds, and offline.
- **Severity:** Minor. It is a misleading note that asks for a Mark that cannot exist and contradicts the Review. Nothing is computed from it.
