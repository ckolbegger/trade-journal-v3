## 4. Minor observations
1. **The receipt heading splits the date from its time at 320:** "POSITION CHANGE AT 2026-10-12 / 10:00". The I2 rule (date and time never split) is written for Execution rows, so this is not a failed condition, but it is the same kind of break.
2. **One-frame overflow on the C2.10 Detail:** right after resizing from 1280 to 390 on the Recovery Only Detail, `scrollWidth` read 434 for one measurement. It reached 390 within 500 ms, in 2 of 4 tries. No settled overflow was seen anywhere, including 97-width sweeps with 30 ms settles.
3. **Validation grammar:** the New Plan issue reads "Enter a intended entry price greater than zero…" ("a intended"; from the template in `plan-issues.ts` line 38). It is older than this batch.
4. **Open Lots table at 1280:** it has a vertical hairline after "Opened" (the pinned-column style), and its bottom border is inset by about 10 px from the card's row edge. This is cosmetic.
5. **DEF row:** a Trade whose stored lifecycle is Planned but which holds 60 shares is listed under Planned as "Long 100 shares @ $50.00 · Records disagree". Its Detail shows the "records disagree" panel (Lifecycle Planned against expected Open) and still offers "Record entry". This only follows from the fixture.
6. **Tab order:** the Planned tab is reached with arrow keys, not Tab, which is the roving-tabindex tab pattern. The storage icon is the first Tab stop on Trades.
7. **Caption scrolls away:** the narrow table caption ("Open Trades") scrolls out of view with the region; Underlying stays pinned.
