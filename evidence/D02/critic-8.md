## 3. Overall: **FAIL**

**Defects**

1. **The Detail summary card's Trade text breaks between "@" and the price at narrow widths (U9c).**
   - **Steps:** confirm a Plan with Quantity 100 and Intended entry 1234, then record 60 @ 1234.50 and 40 @ 1240; open the Trade's Detail at a viewport width of 320 px. Any width up to 340 px fails; with "Long 1000 shares @ $1,500.00" on a Planned Detail, any width up to 350 px fails.
   - **Expected:** U9(c), "a price never wraps away from its '@'"; T2.62, "In the Trade text the '@' and its price are kept together ('Long 120 shares / @ $50.32' is allowed, '… @ / $50.32' is not)"; section 2a says the same `tradeSummary` text is used "by every list and by the Detail summary card".
   - **Actual:** the summary card heading renders "Long 100 shares @" on line 1 and "$1,236.70" on line 2 (captures `test-results/critic-d02-8/r-BIG-320-top.png` and `r-HUGE-320-top.png`).
   - **Cause:** the list cell wraps "@ price" in `span.nowrap` (TradeList.tsx line 191). TradeDetail.tsx line 200 renders `tradeSummary(...)` as plain text. T2.62's measurements cover only Trade cells and Execution rows, so the suite misses it.
   - **Severity:** low. It is cosmetic and nothing is lost, but it fails a U9 pass condition the user decided on.

## 4. Minor observations
1. **Trades caption scrolls away:** on the narrow table, the "Planned Trades" / "Open Trades" caption scrolls out of view when the region is scrolled right. Underlying stays pinned.
2. **About "This release" alignment:** the numbers ("Schema version 2", "Offline assets 6") are right-aligned to the card edge, while the text values start at x 458. It is the same in the committed `17-about-1280.png`, so it was there before U9.
3. **DEF row:** a Trade whose stored lifecycle says Planned but which has 60 shares entered shows "Long 100 shares @ $50.00 · Records disagree" and "Awaiting entry" in the Planned list. The badge flags it, so this is just an observation.
4. **Share quantities:** they are not grouped ("Long 1000 shares", "1000 shares of HUGE"). U9a covers only money.
5. **Long journal answers not tested:** the T2.64 condition "the answer is wider than 480 px when its text needs it" was not exercised; all the answers were short.
6. **Fixture choice:** I first used the `MissingMember` fixture on an Open Trade. As designed (responsive-a11y.spec), it makes the whole Open list show "We couldn't load your open trades." I rebuilt the profile with `UnknownPlannedLeg`. This was my own setup step and is not a defect.
