### 4. Minor observations
1. **Focus drops to the page body after Review actions, so focus is never visible at those moments.** It happens after:
   - pressing a Mark row's Enter (the Price field does not get focus);
   - Save Mark;
   - Continue to Trades;
   - an Action save, where the next Trade's form opens but is not focused.

   Chromium's sequential navigation point is kept, so the next Tab lands on the logical next control and keyboard completion works. Screen-reader users still lose their place. The ui-contract asks for "logical focus after navigation or validation", so this is worth a look. Validation errors do move focus correctly.
2. **The Review status header goes stale after a Mark save.** It keeps "Mark missing for XYZ on Mon Oct 12" until Continue to Trades, even though the row already reads "$52.00 · Manual". This follows A48, but it reads as contradictory.
3. **Mark prices are rounded to 2 decimals.** A Mark saved as 52.123456 shows as "$52.12" in the row and in the Correct form's "Current price $52.12". Entering 52.123456 is then refused with "That is already the Mark", while 52.12 would count as a change. Presentation rounding, but confusing.
4. **The attention reason shows only the side and the share** (for example "31% of the way from entry to the Target"). The daily-review spec says the returned basis also includes "the risk or reward at entry and now". The UI shows neither the at-entry figure nor a labelled "now" figure in the walk; the Trade panel shows only the current figures.
5. **The Review's Trade panel shows no Stop Overrun.** ABC's panel has no Overrun line, though the Detail shows "Breached … $50.00 · 0.25R". Not required by C4.1.
6. **Withdraw takes effect in one click,** with no confirmation and no field for the optional reason A35 allows.
7. **Leaving a half-filled Action form gives no prompt** and discards the values. This is consistent with AC-JOUR-001. Inside the Review, Continue to Trades and the other "Review …" buttons are disabled with "Save your Action or put back its saved values to leave this Trade.", and the Action form offers no Cancel or Revert control for that.
8. **After a Mark is acknowledged, both "Enter" and "Withdraw" are offered.**
9. **About lists `MarketData.query (… ResolveSession …)`,** a variant T4.19's manifest description does not name.
10. **Labels and messages:**
    - The Intent textarea has no `aria-required`; only the label says "(required)".
    - Current figures read "Awaiting the next close" where P12 words it as "Awaiting the Tue Oct 13 close"; B4.14 and T4.23 use the generic wording.
11. **Helpers:**
    - `tabTo` fails when a Tab wraps through the browser chrome to `<body>` (its focus-ring check hits the body). That is helper behavior, not an app defect.
    - Opening a Trade from the Review's Mark row with a link scoped to the row's region did not work: the "Trades: XYZ" link sits outside the "XYZ Mark, …" region. I opened the Trade from the Open list instead.
12. **The shell's warning icon on every page is the storage flag** ("Storage not protected: see About"), not the coverage notice. It has an accessible name.
