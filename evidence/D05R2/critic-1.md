### 4. Minor observations
None of these is a defect. All are in D5R too, unchanged by D5R2.
1. **The opened card's top can end up off-screen.** When an Action save opens the card holding the next Missing Mark and that card is last in the list, its header ends at y = −95. The focused price input is in view at y=556. Header presses still land at y=17 (A151 only states that for header presses).
2. **Card order among tied cards follows the Trade id.** Cards with equal pressure (all "Evidence limited") are ordered by Trade id. So two profiles built the same way can list ABC and JKL in a different order, and the card the next step opens can sit at a different index.
3. **A per-row Correct drops the earlier-optional count.** After the Correct on the Monday XYZ row, T-A's Marks summary changes from "1 of 1 for Mon Oct 12 · 1 earlier optional" to "1 of 1 for Mon Oct 12". The Friday session had been saved earlier and was still listed until then.
4. **After a Save Marks conflict, the panel and the row disagree.** The row shows the other tab's "$53.00 · Manual", but the Marks panel still reads "0 of 1 … / 1 needed", and the blockers still list "Mark missing for XYZ". Pressing Save Marks (2) again just repeats the same conflict, with nothing saved.
5. **An old hold message stays on screen.**
   - After an Action save on a held card, both messages show: "…to leave it." and "The Action is saved… to go on."
   - The "to go on" message is still there after Discard.
6. **Each Open-list "Mark missing" link is in the page twice,** probably once per layout. One copy of each is hidden.
7. **Save Marks stays disabled during the save.** Right after Save Marks is pressed, the button shows "Save Marks (1)" disabled until the panel collapses.
