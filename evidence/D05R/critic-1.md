### 3. Overall: PASS (every C5R flow met its pass condition)

**Defects** (none fails a pass condition):

1. **The Marks panel summary counts the wrong way and reads as "nothing done" after saving.** Severity: low (wording).
   - **Steps:** open T-A with no Monday Mark. The Marks header reads "1 of 1 for Mon Oct 12 · 1 earlier optional" with status "1 needed". Save the Monday Mark. It now reads "0 of 1 for Mon Oct 12 · 1 earlier optional" with "Saved". The same happens on every card ("0 of 1 for Mon Oct 12 · Saved").
   - **Expected:** A152's example pairs "1 of 4 for Mon Oct 12" with "3 needed", so "N of M" counts what is done.
   - **Actual:** N counts what is still needed, so a fully saved panel says "0 of 1".

2. **Focus is lost after pressing "Reflect on …" in the Reflections panel.** Severity: low (accessibility).
   - **Steps:** with the keyboard, focus "Reflect on 2026-10-09 10:00" in T-A's Reflections panel and press Enter.
   - **Expected:** focus stays logical after the form opens (UI contract: "Keyboard focus is visible and logical"; "logical focus after navigation or validation").
   - **Actual:** `document.activeElement` becomes BODY. The next Tab does reach the form's first radio, so keyboard users can carry on, but a screen reader loses its place. This may come from D5's DebtForm, which this panel reuses.

### 4. Minor observations
- **Card header scrolled out of view when focus goes into a card.** After an Action save, after following a Missing-Mark link, and after a blocker or `?instrument=` link, the card opens and focus goes to its price input. The browser scrolls just enough to show the input, which leaves the card's header above the viewport (y = −223 px for ABC after the Action save; −262 px for T-F from the Open list). The trader can't see which position they are in without scrolling up, though the "Today's Marks" row names the Instrument. Opening a card by pressing its header does keep the header in view (y = 17 px), as A151 requires. Nothing in A151 or A161 requires the header to be visible for these paths.
- **Next card after an Action save.** It was ABC (second in attention order), not DEF (first), when both had a Missing Mark. This matches the blockers' Instrument order and P36's "prefers a card with a Missing current Mark", so it is not a defect.
- **Navigating away discards drafts.** With an unsaved Mark draft, clicking "Trades" in the navigation leaves the Review and the draft is lost, with no prompt (nothing is written). A156 says "route changes and the update notice wait", but nothing holds a route change: unsaved state is only reported to the update notice. The spec only requires that navigation without Save records nothing, which holds.
- **Blocker links don't update the URL.** Clicking "Action missing for XYZ (Long 50 shares @ $50.00)" opens T-F's Action panel, but the address stays `/review`. A reload then opens the first unfinished card (T-A), not T-F. The link's own `href` (`?trade=…&panel=Action`) works if opened directly.
- **Recovery Only "Reflect on" button.** The button stays enabled and opens the Reflection form with every input disabled and only Cancel. Nothing can be saved, but A162 says inputs are "not offered".
- **Planned Reward : risk format.** It shows as a bare number ("3", "10", "2.4", "2.5"), the same as T5R.11 expects. A ratio form such as "2.4 : 1" might read more clearly next to the "Reward : risk" label.
- **Helper misuse on my side.** `answerDebt(page, card)` timed out because it expects the Debt form to be open and answered already, which my call didn't do. Answering by hand with no choices shows "Answer "Decision mode"." and "Answer "Primary emotion"." beside the prompts; Decline worked. This is not a product finding.
