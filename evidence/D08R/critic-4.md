## 4. Minor observations (none rises to a defect)
1. **Where the date sits in a dated row.** In Plan and Risk's Conditions, a row from the last Marks carries the date only once, in the first dated cell. On Sat 10-17 that is "$2.20 away · Thu Oct 15 Marks", while "Risk to stop $220.00 · 1.10R" in the same row has no date. When only the call Mark is missing, the date moves to the Risk cell instead ("Reward to target $180.00 · 0.90R · Thu Oct 15 Marks"). This is consistent with W31 read per row, but the date lands in a different column depending on the case.
2. **Empty track name.** On O-S2X, which has no option-price condition, the Today option track's accessible name is "Option price:", with nothing after the colon (seen Fri Nov 20 12:00). This is D08P's track, not a D8R change.
3. **Narrow Conditions table.** At 320 px the Plan and Risk Conditions table scrolls sideways inside its own box ("Conditions table" region), so the Risk and Value at expiration columns are cut until scrolled. The page itself does not scroll sideways.
4. **Expired date without a year.** For an option that expired in an earlier year, the "Expired" cell's accessible name is "Expired Fri Dec 19", with no year, when viewed in Jan 2026. This is a date, not a contract name, so W28 and P117 don't cover it.
5. **Reason names the call when XYZ is missing too.** At Mon Oct 12 18:00 with no Marks at all, the Underlying condition's Plan and Risk cells read "Mark missing: XYZ Dec 18 $55 Call, Mon Oct 12", although XYZ's own Mark is also missing. This is the existing reason selection.
6. **Breached row keeps the role.** The rail's breached row reads "Stop · Breached · $0.50 past", with the role before W21's "Breached · … past". That seems intended.
7. **Tab after paging.** On the Journal's last page, with no buttons in its cards, Tab from "Newer sessions" goes next to the "Trades" link in the primary navigation.
8. **Settlement Due Underlying dashes.** On Settlement Due, MNO's Today cell is "—" because the Review never asks for MNO's Mark. This is the accepted revision 98 future capability.
