## 3. Overall: PASS
C8R.1 to C8R.4 meet their pass conditions on the scenarios they name. The three defects below come from states next to those scenarios: a Settlement Due Trade and a stock Trade.

**Defects**

1. **Plan and Risk shows "Since the last Marks" when no lots were bought after earlier Marks** — **Moderate**
   - **Steps:**
     - Take O-S2X (2 short puts sold Mon 10-12 10:00) and enter no Marks. Open Trade Detail at Fri 11-20 12:00, then open Plan and Risk.
     - Or take the copy with the Fri 11-20 Marks and open Plan and Risk at Mon 11-23 18:00, when the Trade is Settlement Due.
   - **Expected:** no such group. W23 says it is "Shown only when lots were bought after earlier Marks". T8R.12 says it should not show before the first Marks, when Entry Quality covers the entry. Here Entry Quality covers the entry ("Resolved"). In the second case the last Marks (Fri Nov 20) came after the lots.
   - **Actual:** "Since the last Marks: New lots 2 contracts · Average price of new lots $1.25 · Risk from that price $350.00 · 0.97R · Reward from that price $250.00 · 0.69R". The words claim new lots that don't exist. The figures also repeat Entry Quality's risk and reward from the entry.
   - **For contrast:** an Open stock Trade before its first Marks, and the same put at Fri 11-20 18:00 after its Marks, correctly leave the group out.

2. **Plan and Risk measures a Settlement Due contract's conditions that the rail and the note say are no longer measured** — **Moderate**
   - **Steps:** take O-S2X with the Fri 11-20 Marks saved. Open Trade Detail at Fri 11-20 18:30 (after the 4:00 pm expiration; Needs you shows the Settlement Due panel). Open Plan and Risk.
   - **Expected:** the rows give the reason, not figures, as they do on Mon 11-23, where every Distance and Risk or reward cell reads "Settlement Due: MNO Nov 20 $45 Put". The rail agrees, showing role and trigger only (W21).
   - **Actual:** the Conditions table shows "2.00 away · Risk to stop $520.00 · 1.44R" and "6.00 away · Reward to target $80.00 · 0.22R". Directly underneath it says "The contract expired, so its conditions are no longer measured." The rail on the same screen shows no distances.

3. **On a stock Trade, the Conditions table's Condition cell shows only a bare price** — **Moderate**
   - **Steps:** open the ABC stock Trade's Plan and Risk at Mon 10-12 12:00 (before its first Marks), and again at Mon 18:00.

## 4. Minor observations
1. **Payoff repeats figures.** It shows "including +$98.70 realized to date" and "If XYZ is at 57.90 at expiration: +$147.40". The first repeats Position's Realized to date; the second repeats Today's XYZ Mark. At Fri 12:00 that 57.90 is Thursday's Mark with no date. These are D08P's W8 words and not D8R's, but they read inconsistently. 3. **Missing period in W24.** The Journal's Deviation line has no final period after the button ("1 active Deviation. See Plan and Risk"); W24's text ends "See Plan and Risk.".
4. **An owed reflection in the Journal uses the old form.** The Wednesday card reads "Position Change at 2026-10-14 11:00", then the term "Due" with the value "Due". It is an obligation, not an entry, but it is the one item on that card not in W27's form.
5. **Daily Trade Review heading.** It keeps "Action: Hold" as a separate row. The audit's mockup puts it in the heading ("Daily Trade Review · Hold"); the breakdown's A239 allows either.
6. **Tables cramped at 375 px.** The Conditions table needs a sideways scroll inside its region at 375 px, so the "Value at expiration" column ($5.00) is off-screen until scrolled. There is a fade cue, and the region is focusable and labelled.

8. **Closed-by-sale row label.** The Today row reads "Call Mark" for the exit price, $3.40. This follows A235 as approved, but the user's rule is that a price is a Mark, so the label is worth confirming.

10. **Unlabelled dated figures.** When today's Mark is missing, Plan and Risk puts the session date on the Distance cell but not on the Risk or reward cell in the same row (for example "Risk to stop $220.00 · 1.10R" beside "$2.20 away · Thu Oct 15 Marks").
