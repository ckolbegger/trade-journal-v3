Trade Detail audit

Which parts of the Trade Detail page are left over from the original build, which are in the wrong view, and what each view should show instead. Recommendations only: nothing in the app has been changed. Written 2026-11-14, from the code at ca839e70.

In short

  • You were right about the leftovers. The cockpit still shows several cards from the old page: the At entry card, the dated "At the <date> Marks" card, an old Valuation card ("Expected Mark Date", Mark, Source, Saved), some Summary-card rows, and a long block of details on every Journal entry. They weren't missed by accident. The D08P plan kept each of them "in its existing words", so the page now shows many figures twice.
  • Deviations are on every view. They sit, with Planned-Leg Fulfillment, in a footer under every drawer. The plan and the prototype both put them there. Per your answers, both move into Plan and Risk as its last two groups, and a short line in the Journal points to them.
  • The Position drawer doesn't say which contract each lot is. The Open Lots and Lot Matches tables have no Instrument column. That matters most once a Trade has two legs. Both tables gain one, and every contract is named in the broker style, "NVDA Dec 18 $150 Call", everywhere in the app.
  • Today becomes one simple Marks table: Today, Yesterday and Change, one row per Instrument. Every Trade except a Planned one, or one whose records disagree, gets a Today view, including Settlement Due and Closed Trades (greyed, with a "Closed" stamp).
  • The rail's Conditions card shows distances, not dollars. It shows how far each price is from its trigger ("Stop · $2.20 away"). Dollars at risk, with their R, Worst case and Max reward move to Plan and Risk, so each dollar figure has one home.
  • Three backend additions are needed, all small and additive: a price distance to each trigger (Trade Analysis), the Underlying's two Marks on a Settlement Due Trade (Trade Views), and the open lots' average price for an option Trade (Trade Views). One field becomes unused and should be removed: the "nearest" flag.

The prioritised list of changes is at the end, in Recommended changes, in order.

1. How this was checked

I read the D08P plan and its two handoffs, the "C. Cockpit" tab of the prototype (all ten of its Trade states), the cockpit code, the decisions and revisions that touch Trade Detail, and the specs named in the brief. Then I seeded journals through the app's own first run, Plan form, entry and exit forms and Daily Review, and took a screenshot of every view in each of these states:

  • a Planned option Trade, both just confirmed and after a reload;
  • an option Trade entered before any Marks;
  • an option Trade held for four sessions, with a partial exit, a Reflection owed and one session without Marks;
  • the same Trade after one more contract was bought the next day, which also gives an Entry Size Deviation;
  • a stock Trade with a Deviation, first with its Mark missing, then with its Stop breached;
  • a plain held stock Trade, and a stock Trade Closed by sale;
  • a Cash-Secured Put that is Settlement Due, then Closed by expiration.

The state where the records disagree wasn't reached, because it needs a direct database edit. It is covered from the code and the prototype only.

Technical detail: how the app was served

No other server was started and no other port was used. Instead, the test browser answered every request to http://localhost:5175 from a copy of the built release on disk (dist/, built after the last src commit, on a clean tree), through Playwright's context.route. Data was seeded by calling the e2e support functions (buildOc3, createTrades-style steps, recordOptionEntry, enterMark, recordExpiration), not their fixtures.

Other problems found along the way

  • Before the first Marks, the page contradicts itself. The note says the first Marks are "due once the Mon Oct 12 session ends". But Today shows "Expected Mark Date Fri Oct 9", and Today's distance, Position's Open P&L and Plan and Risk's figures all say "Awaiting the close after Fri Oct 9" (open-option-before-marks--*.png). The new Today table removes Expected Mark Date. The remaining "Awaiting the close after Fri Oct 9" figures should name the session that brings the first Marks, the one in the note.
  • The option track's "entry" dot ignores later lots. It sits at Entry Quality's average ($2.00) even after a lot was bought at $3.30. It should use the open lots' average (see Today).
  • Three different averages on one stock Trade. The Summary line and At entry say $50.32, and Entry Quality says $50.18. They measure different things, but they share a label. The "Since the last Marks" group's new row names ("Average price of new lots") remove the clash.
  • A breached Stop reads "$0.00 · 0.00R" in Plan and Risk while the rail says "Breached". Plan and Risk should show how far past it is, "$60.00 · 0.24R past".

8. Today

What changes: Today becomes one Marks table, with three columns, Today, Yesterday and Change, and one row per Instrument. Each column is headed with its date. When a Mark hasn't been entered yet, its cell shows a dash, so a glance tells you what is still missing. The current-Marks card, the dated card, the At entry card, the per-condition lines and the extra Mark-missing link all leave. Notes about a stale Mark or an acknowledged missing Mark appear under the table, only when they apply. Open Trades keep the price tracks; Settlement Due and Closed Trades don't.

Which days the columns show

  • Today: the current trading day, with dashes until its Marks are entered after the session ends. On a weekend or holiday, it is the next trading session's date.
  • Yesterday: always the session just before the Today column, with dashes if that session has no Marks. It never skips back to an earlier session that has Marks.
  • Change: Today's Mark less Yesterday's, or a dash when either is missing.
  • Before the first Marks: both columns show dashes, beside the "No Marks yet" note.

Mid-session, before the first Marks, and notes

Settlement Due

The Underlying keeps trading after expiration, and its Mark tells the trader whether the option finished in or out of the money. So Today shows the Underlying's Marks for today and yesterday, where entered. The option has no Marks after its expiration session, so its cells read "Expired" rather than a dash, which would look like a missing Mark.

One consequence of revision 98. Revision 98 ended a Settlement Due Trade's history at its expiration session, and the Daily Review no longer asks for any Marks once the contract has expired, including the Underlying's. So the Today column will usually stay a dash on a Settlement Due Trade, unless the trader enters the Underlying's Mark some other way. You chose to keep the columns literal; the dated headings make the dashes self-explaining. If the trader should see the Underlying's latest price there, the Review would need to keep asking for the Underlying's Mark until the expiration is recorded. That would reverse part of revision 98, so it isn't recommended here, only noted.

Closed

The whole Today view is greyed, with a "Closed" stamp across it. The Today column is blank, because today's Marks no longer matter. The second column shows the price the position closed at, from the final exit, or "Expired" for an expiration, headed with that date. It never calls a Mark a "close". Change shows dashes, since nothing is measured. No price tracks.

Technical detail
  • Where the Open table's figures come from: the replay. Its completed sessions give each Instrument's Mark and its change since that Instrument's previous Mark (today.props.ts:26–57 already reads them). Change is shown only when the change's since date is the Yesterday column's date, so nothing is worked out in the UI. The session not yet ended (the date of the Journal's "Session not ended yet" card, W12) gives the Today column's date, including the next session's date on a weekend.
  • Settlement Due needs a backend addition. An expired contract's Detail reads no Mark frame (revision 73), and its replay stops at the expiration session (revision 98). So the Underlying's two Marks after expiration aren't available to the screen. See What the backend must add.
  • Closed by expiration: the replay of a Closed Trade ends at its final session, so the Underlying's Mark on the expiration date is already there. The exit column's price and date are the final exit's, taken from the Position Changes the view already holds. Choosing the last one is selection, not arithmetic.
  • Entry dot and ticks: the open lots' prices are already in positions[].openLots. Their average is a derived figure, so it must come from Trade Views, not the UI (ADR 0001). A stock Trade already has one in its Trade Summary price; an option Trade needs a small additive field.
  • Code that leaves: CurrentMarksCard, CurrentMarksOnly and FigureCard (today.tsx:58–103, 155–156); valuedAtOf, atEntryOf, currentMarksOf and conditionLineOf (today.props.ts:130–144, 184–213, 233–273); the shell's CurrentMarksOnly branch (Cockpit.tsx:105).

9. Position, and naming contracts

What changes: every contract is named on every row. Open Lots and Lot Matches each gain an Instrument column, the Position row becomes one row per leg, and the Opening fees row goes (the Fee reconciliation already shows it). The Trade Summary line, Net cost and Last activity stay. Every contract name everywhere in the app takes the broker style, "NVDA Dec 18 $150 Call", in place of "NVDA 18 Dec 2026 150 Call".

The prototype never named the contract on the Position card, and nor does the built Open Lots table. On a one-leg Trade the Trade Summary line covers it. Two-leg Trades come right after D8, and then a lots table without names can't be read. Executions and Settlement already name the contract on each line, so they only need the new style.

Technical detail
  • Open Lots: columns at src/ui/cockpit/drawers/position.props.ts:38–41. Each lot belongs to a position whose instrument is known (view.positions).
  • Lot Matches: columns at src/ui/screens/LotMatchesTable.tsx:48–53. It is shared with ExitReceipt.tsx and ExpirationReceipt.tsx.
  • Position row: position.props.ts:32 joins positions with ", ". It becomes one row per leg.
  • Executions and Settlement already name the contract (src/ui/screens/execution-line.ts:14; trade-detail/Position.tsx:90, 154, 165).
  • The contract name comes from one place: instrumentLabel (src/ui/calculation-words.ts:22–25), with about 20 call sites in 14 files (Trade Detail, the entry and expiration forms, Mark entry, the Review card, ReviewSettlement). The short form already exists in the Trade Summary line (src/ui/format.ts:46–63, summaryExpiration and summaryStrike), so instrumentLabel can reuse it with the symbol in front. 47 test and e2e files contain long-form dates; each needs checking, since some are the "Expires" line rather than a contract name.
  • Opening fees: remove the row at position.props.ts:34. The C8P.2 exception for it goes.

10. Plan and Risk

What changes: Plan and Risk becomes the one home for the Plan, every dollar figure about risk and reward, the entry, and Deviations. In order:

  1. Plan, unchanged: still a complete record, including Stop, Target and Account.
  2. Plan Baseline, unchanged.
  3. Conditions, new here: one row per condition with its trigger, its distance, and its Risk to stop or Reward to target in dollars and R. An Underlying condition on an option Trade also shows its value at expiration. Worst case, Max reward and the short notes sit under the rows.
  4. Entry Quality, unchanged.
  5. Since the last Marks, new: shown only when lots were bought after earlier Marks.
  6. Planned-Leg Fulfillment, moved from the footer.
  7. Deviations, moved from the footer, and hidden when there are none.
Technical detail
  • planRiskProps (src/ui/cockpit/drawers/plan-risk.props.ts:105–118) gains the conditions group (replacing conditionFiguresGroup, lines 48–64), the "Since the last Marks" group (from evaluation.atEntry), and the two groups from footer.props.ts.
  • The dollar figures come from each condition's existing distance and overrun Measures (A206), the distance from the new trigger field, and the value at expiration from baseline.stopBasis and targetBasis (as today.props.ts:132–143 reads it now).
  • Worst case and Max reward move from conditions.props.ts:106–107, in W6's words.
  • When today's Mark is missing, the figures come from current.datedLine.evaluation, labelled with its date.
  • The footer component (src/ui/cockpit/footer.tsx, footer.props.ts, footer.types.ts) and the shell's DetailFooter (Cockpit.tsx:168) leave.
