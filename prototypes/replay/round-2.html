Trade replay, round 2: six ways to draw it

A prototype for D9. The same six trades as round 1 (round 1 is kept here). Each visualization shows the price and the Trade’s profit or loss together, and both move with the slider. It opens on the last session; drag the slider left to replay.

A model, not market data. The Underlying’s Marks are written by hand and every option Mark is generated from them by a pricing model, for this demonstration only. The generator is at the foot of the page.
