About this mockup

  • New: one meter per row, under the P&L figure (inside the Trade cell on a phone). Everything else is the Open list as it is today.
  • The track runs from the Trade's result at the Stop to its result at the Target, so every row has the same ends and rows can be compared down the list. Where the break-even tick sits shows the reward to risk: nearer the left means more reward than risk.
  • Hover a meter for its figures. A screen reader hears the same words.
  • Order is a mockup control. The app's Open list is in Last Activity order today; Attention is the Daily Review's order, where the meter explains why a Trade is near the top.
  • Twelve made-up Trades, Marked at the Fri Nov 13 close. Rows worth a look: DALE (past the Stop), HELO (past the Target), GALT (half sold: the Stop now locks in a profit), LUMA (a banked profit and an open loss), IRIS (no Mark), JUNO (entered that day).
