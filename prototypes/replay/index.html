Trade replay: six ways to draw it

A prototype for D9. Six visualizations of the same six trades. Choose a trade and a visualization, then move the slider from left to right: each step reveals one more session, and every figure is as of that session.

A model, not market data. The Underlying's Marks on this page are written by hand, and every option Mark is generated from them by a pricing model (Black-Scholes), for this demonstration only. In the app, Marks are observed and nothing is generated. The generator is at the foot of the page.
