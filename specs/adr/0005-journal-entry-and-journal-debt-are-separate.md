# Journal Entry and Journal Debt are separate

Status: accepted

A Journal Entry is immutable saved trader writing. Journal Debt is a durable obligation to provide or explicitly decline writing later. Debt is not a blank, draft, pending, or mutable Entry and may validly retire without fabricating journal content.

Placeholder Entries were rejected because they blur authored evidence with work owed and make decline or retirement look like writing. Settling Debt by answer atomically creates an Entry from the original snapshotted prompts and marks that Debt Settled. Decline and valid retirement remain distinct durable outcomes. Due Debt blocks Daily Review completion but never blocks factual Trade capture.

Ordinary Entry corrections append a version under the same Entry identity. Addenda are distinct later Entries. Voids remain visible and reopen Debt when the Voided Entry was its sole settlement.
