# Corrected economic history with visible audit

Status: accepted

Calculations use the currently effective corrected facts at their original economic times. The product does not offer a second full history reconstructed by record-save time. Replace, Void, and Rebuild are append-versioned and remain visible through ordinary Edit and View history experiences.

Replace means a real event was recorded incorrectly. Void means the recorded event never happened. Rebuild makes a coherent reconstructed record effective under the same economic Trade identity. Corrections restate affected calculations at the original economic date and return a structured Correction Footprint containing the fact, save time, affected domains, and affected replay interval.

This avoids both destructive overwrites and a costly bitemporal valuation model. Saved prior values remain transparent audit and coaching-ready evidence, but drafts, keystrokes, and abandoned edits are never captured.
