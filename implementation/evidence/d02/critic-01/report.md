## Manual accessibility and presentation observations

The cream/white surface, semantic badges and right-aligned financial values are readable. Status/error text supplies meaning without color. Buttons have clear labels, radio options sit inside named groups, validation directs focus to its summary, and route headings receive visible focus. No scale-in, exit, Marks, Edit, Restore, reports or later-feature placeholder controls appear.

Measured text contrast ranges13.56–14.97:1; input boundary4.42:1; focus outline5.43:1 on white and4.94:1 on cream. Those checks complement axe, rather than claiming that every native widget pixel was exhaustively measured.

**Usability observation:** retaining Note focus during a1440→390 live resize can place part of the textarea behind the fixed bottom navigation. The settled capture `focus-390-settled.png` and corresponding JSON show textarea y898.45–998.45 versus nav y921.84–1000 in390×1000. Its top outline remains visible, no horizontal overflow occurs, ordinary scrolling exposes the content, and keyboard completion passed. This is worth considering for scroll padding/focus comfort. I do not claim it is an axe failure or an entirely obscured-focus WCAG2.2 failure. Parent reviewed the UI/acceptance contracts and agreed this is a usability observation rather than a demonstrated contract failure because focus remains visible, scrolling and keyboard completion remain available, and values/actions are preserved. It remains present; no D2 implementation change is being made for it.
